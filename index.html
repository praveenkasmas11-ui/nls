About the Author

Their Stories, Our Truth

Iqra, April, Luna, Margo, Turquoise, and Zina—six women from Mangalore navigating love, loss, rage, and the relentless weight of existing in a man's world.

Each carries her own storm: the terror of unsafe spaces, the ache of chronic pain, the betrayal of shattered dreams, the suffocation of expectations. April is conflicted between finding the one and shattering glass ceilings. Turquoise's tummy hurts and she hates the world. Iqra has irrevocably fallen in love, and Zina's worldview is profoundly shattering. Luna is fearfully avoiding her uncle, and Margo suddenly starts throwing up one day.

Nine Lives of Sophia is a bold, unflinching exploration of womanhood—raw, rebellious, and achingly real. As their lives interweave across time and memory, one haunting question emerges: Can a woman ever be truly free?

This novel is both a mirror and a rebellion—a stirring and poignant tale woven across time, epistemologies, and people, revealing just how shared the oppressive patriarchal experience truly is.

About Naiema Hanif

Naiema grew up in the coastal city of Mangalore amidst the Western Ghats and the Arabian Sea. She obsesses over affordable Swiss furniture, loves plush toys, hates the monkeys on her balcony, laments about the apathy in the world, the capitalistic order of things, and the caging patriarchy that refuses to let go.

Naiema began writing as a teen, though this is her first published book. In another life, she might've been a journalist or a lawyer; in this one, she holds up an unflinching mirror to life through her words.

She is currently pursuing her Master's in Arts, working at an AI startup, building an invisible disabilities advocacy community, and is an aspiring professor.
